How it works

Many people already brainstorm, rewrite and second-guess their posts in a chatbot. Content OS gives that process a proper home, with your notes, drafts, feedback and pictures connected in one place.

  1. Discover

    Start from a thought, notes or a draft. Or point it at your notes, voice memos and reading, and pick from the ideas it finds.

  2. Develop

    It finds the angle and writes the draft. When the point isn’t clear yet, it offers a few to choose from.

  3. Create

    It shapes the post for LinkedIn or X and picks a picture from your own photos.

  4. Review

    It checks facts, argument and voice, and marks what needs a second look.
